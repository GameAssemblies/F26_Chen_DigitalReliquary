**Digital Reliquary investigates how the spatial and perceptual qualities of sacred architecture can be translated into a continuously evolving digital environment using real-time generative AI, TouchDesigner, sound, and audience input.**


**Research topic:** sacred architecture → responsive digital space
**Technical research:** real-time image generation + feedback
**Conceptual research**: 
- How to translate sacred qualities of architectural/visual arts to the digital space?
- Does an interactive environment need to respond immediately?
